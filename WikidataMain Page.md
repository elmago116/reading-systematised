Welcome!

Wikidata is a free and open knowledge base that can be read and edited by both humans and machines.

Wikidata acts as central storage for the **structured data** of its Wikimedia sister projects including Wikipedia, Wikivoyage, Wiktionary, Wikisource, and others.

Wikidata also provides support to many other sites and services beyond just Wikimedia projects! The content of Wikidata is [available under a free license](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), [exported using standard formats](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Special:MyLanguage/Wikidata:Database_download "Special:MyLanguage/Wikidata:Database download"), and [can be interlinked to other open data sets](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Special:MyLanguage/Wikidata:Data_access "Special:MyLanguage/Wikidata:Data access") on the linked data web.

Get involved

***Contribute to Wikidata***

- Learn to edit Wikidata: follow the **[tutorials](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Special:MyLanguage/Wikidata:Tours "Special:MyLanguage/Wikidata:Tours")**.
- Work with other volunteers on a subject that interests you: **[join a WikiProject](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Special:MyLanguage/Wikidata:WikiProjects "Special:MyLanguage/Wikidata:WikiProjects")**.
- Individuals and organizations can also **[donate data](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Special:MyLanguage/Wikidata:Data_donation "Special:MyLanguage/Wikidata:Data donation")**.

***Meet the Wikidata community***

- Visit the **[community portal](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Special:MyLanguage/Wikidata:Community_portal "Special:MyLanguage/Wikidata:Community portal")** or attend a **[Wikidata event](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Special:MyLanguage/Wikidata:Events "Special:MyLanguage/Wikidata:Events")**.
- Create a **[user account](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Special:CreateAccount "Special:CreateAccount")**.
- Talk and ask questions on the **[Project chat](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Special:MyLanguage/Wikidata:Project_chat "Special:MyLanguage/Wikidata:Project chat")**, **[Telegram groups](https://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:MyLanguage/Telegram#Wikidata "meta:Special:MyLanguage/Telegram")**, or the **[live IRC chat](irc://irc.libera.chat/wikidata)** <sup><a href="https://web.libera.chat/?channel=#wikidata">connect</a></sup>.

News

- 📢 [WikiProject Days 2026](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Special:MyLanguage/Event:WikiProjects_Days_2026 "Special:MyLanguage/Event:WikiProjects Days 2026") took place from June 19 to 21 online. We talked about how WikiProjects can be used as a space to connect with others and inform data modelling and quality decisions. Sessions included how to make Wikiprojects more visible, create guidelines for good WikiProjects, and bring life back into neglected WikiProjects. Everyone was welcome. Sessions were recorded. You can watch them from Wikimedia Commons and take a look at the presentation slides used in the event.
- Mobile editors can now edit all datatypes directly in the mobile view. To help improve this new interface, Wikimedia Deutschland is looking for volunteers (especially editors from right-to-left languages) for UX testing sessions. Participants will be compensated for their time. [Sign up here](https://greatquestion.co/wikimediadeutschland/bo2e7e2a/apply) and learn more on the project page: [Mobile editing of statements](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Special:MyLanguage/Wikidata:Usability_and_usefulness/Item_editing_experience/Mobile_editing_of_statements "Special:MyLanguage/Wikidata:Usability and usefulness/Item editing experience/Mobile editing of statements").
- 2025-11-12: [Temporary accounts](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Special:MyLanguage/Help:Temporary_accounts "Special:MyLanguage/Help:Temporary accounts") were deployed on Wikidata. From now on unregistered editors will get a temporary account automatically assigned to them, instead of being identified by their IP addresses.
